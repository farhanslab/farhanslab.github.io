# Privacy Policy — Deen-e-Hayat

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Deen-e-Hayat ("the app") is developed by Farhan's Lab ("we"). This policy explains what information the app
handles and why.

## The short version

- **Your location never leaves your phone.** If you choose to use it, it is used on your device only, to calculate
  prayer times and the Qibla direction. We never upload, collect, store on a server or share it.
- The app has no accounts and no sign-in. The Qur'an, hadith and duas are built into the app and work offline.
- The app uses Google services for **usage analytics**, **crash reports** and **advertising**. These collect
  device and usage information, never your location from the app, your settings or anything you type.

## Information that stays on your device

- **Location.** The app asks for approximate (not precise) location only when you tap the button to use your location,
  and reads it once. It is used to calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction and your nearest district, and to correct
  the compass. It is saved only in the app's private storage, is excluded from Android backups and device transfers,
  and is never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. You can choose one of Bangladesh's 64 districts instead.
- **Prayer settings** (calculation method, Asr method, which prayer reminders are on). Saved on your phone only.
- **Tasbih counts** (current count, target, rounds and lifetime total). Saved on your phone only.
- **App settings** (language, theme, accent colour). Saved on your phone only.
- **Zakat calculator amounts** you type are kept in memory while you use the calculator and are never saved or sent.
- **Prayer reminders** are scheduled on your phone as local notifications. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.
- **Home screen widgets** show prayer times, your tasbih count and the Qibla direction using the same on-device data.
- **Images you save or share** (a Qur'an verse, dua or hadith as a card) are created on your phone and go only where you
  choose to save or share them. On Android 9 and older, saving asks for storage permission.

Settings and tasbih counts (but not your location) may be included in your own Android backup, which Android encrypts
and stores in your Google account if backups are turned on. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of
the information above from your phone.

## Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. They may collect information automatically. None of them receive your
location from the app, your settings, your tasbih counts or the amounts you type.

| Service | Purpose | Information collected |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase Analytics | Understand how the app is used, to improve it | App instance ID, advertising ID, device model, OS version, app version, approximate location (country / region, from IP address), language, and automatic app usage events: app opens, sessions, screens viewed, installs, updates and ad impressions |
| Google Firebase Crashlytics | Find and fix crashes | Crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, installation identifier, device state at the time of the crash |
| Google AdMob | Show ads, which keep the app free | Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions. Ads may be personalised where you have allowed it |

Ads appear only as banners at the bottom of the Home, Qibla and Tasbih screens. There are no ads on the Qur'an and
hadith screens.

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites  
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy  
Firebase privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

**Gold and silver prices.** The Zakat calculator downloads the current BAJUS gold and silver prices from gold-price.bd.
These requests contain no personal information, but like any website, gold-price.bd can see your IP address.

We do not sell your personal information. We receive analytics and crash reports only in aggregated or
pseudonymous form, and use them only to improve the app.

## Your choices

- **Location:** optional. You can deny the permission and pick a district instead, or turn the permission off at any
  time in your phone's settings.
- **Prayer reminders:** turn each reminder on or off in the app, or turn off notifications in your phone's settings.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset it or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings
  (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- **Consent:** users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent when the app first
  starts. Ads, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics stay off until that choice is known, and then follow it. You can change
  your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options.
- **Delete local data:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
- **Analytics data:** contact us with your request; analytics data is not linked to your name or email.

## Data retention

Google Analytics keeps event-level data (individual app events) for 2 months and user-level data (linked to the
app instance or advertising ID) for 14 months, and then deletes it automatically. Aggregated reports, which do not
identify anyone, may be kept longer.
Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Security

All information sent to Google services and gold-price.bd is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
