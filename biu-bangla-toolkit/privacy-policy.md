# Privacy Policy — বিU: Bangla Toolkit

Effective date: 4 November 2026

বিU: Bangla Toolkit ("the app") is developed by Farhan's Lab ("we"). This policy explains what information the app
handles and why.

## The short version

- **Your text never leaves your phone.** Everything you type, paste or convert is processed on your device only.
  We never upload, collect, store on a server or share your text.
- The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
- The app uses Google services for **usage analytics**, **crash reports** and **advertising**. These collect
  device and usage information, never your text.

## Information that stays on your device

- **Text you enter** in any tool, and its results. It is kept in memory while you use the tool and is not sent anywhere.
- **Conversion history** (Bijoy ↔ Unicode). Saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the
  app or clearing its data deletes it.
- **Settings** (language, theme). Saved on your phone only.
- **Clipboard.** The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste, and writes to it only when you tap Copy.
- **Images you save or share** are created on your phone and go only where you choose to save or share them.

## Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. They may collect information automatically. None of them receive the
text you type or convert.

| Service | Purpose | Information collected |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase Analytics | Understand how the app is used, to improve it | App instance ID, advertising ID, device model, OS version, app version, approximate location (country / region, from IP address), language, and app usage events: which screens are opened and which tool actions are used (copy, share, paste) — never the text itself |
| Google Firebase Crashlytics | Find and fix crashes | Crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, installation identifier, device state at the time of the crash |
| Google AdMob | Show ads, which keep the app free | Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions. Ads may be personalised where you have allowed it |

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites  
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy  
Firebase privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We do not sell your personal information. We receive analytics and crash reports only in aggregated or
pseudonymous form, and use them only to improve the app.

## Your choices

- **Advertising ID:** you can reset it or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings
  (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- **Consent:** users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised
  ads are shown, and can change their choice in the app.
- **Delete local data:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
- **Analytics data:** contact us with your request; analytics data is not linked to your name or email.

## Data retention

Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics (up to 14 months) and then deleted.
Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Security

All information sent to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
